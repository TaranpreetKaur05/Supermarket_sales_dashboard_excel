# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze supermarket performance for the **first quarter of 2019 (January–March)**.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence report, allowing users to analyze **sales, quantity sold, gross income, customer ratings, branches, product lines, payment methods, customer types, gender, and monthly performance**.

The project is designed to provide a clear view of overall business performance while helping identify trends, high-performing categories, branch-level differences, and areas requiring attention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall supermarket sales performance.
- Compare sales and quantity across different branches.
- Identify the highest-performing product lines.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare customer types and purchasing behavior.
- Examine customer ratings across branches.
- Analyze product-level revenue, quantity, and gross income.
- Create an interactive dashboard for easier business analysis.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of key KPIs:

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Gross Income
- Average Customer Rating
- Tax Paid

Interactive filters are available for:

- Branch
- Product Line
- Payment Method
- Gender

### 2. Branch Analysis
Compares the performance of Branches A, B, and C using:

- Total Sales
- Quantity Sold
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Unit Price

The analysis highlights differences between sales volume and revenue across branches.

### 3. Product Analysis
Provides product-line level analysis covering:

- Total Sales
- Quantity Sold
- Gross Income
- Unit Price

This section helps identify the contribution of different product categories to overall supermarket performance.

### 4. Deeper Analysis
Provides additional business insights through:

- Monthly sales analysis
- Branch-wise sales comparison
- Customer-type analysis
- Quantity sold by customer type
- Monthly quantity trends
- Product-level performance

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

- **Branch C** records the highest total sales among the three branches.
- **Branch A** records the highest quantity sold, showing that higher sales volume does not necessarily result in the highest revenue.
- **Branch B** has the lowest average customer rating among the three branches.
- **Sports and Travel** generates the highest total sales among the product lines.
- **Food and Beverages** generates the highest gross income.
- **Electronic Accessories** records the highest quantity sold among the product lines.
- **January** records the highest monthly sales, while **February** records the lowest.
- Customer purchasing behavior can also be compared between **Members and Normal customers** across different branches.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard converts transactional supermarket data into a structured analytical report that can help stakeholders understand **where revenue is being generated, which product categories contribute most to performance, how branches differ, and how sales change over time**.

Instead of relying on raw transaction records, decision-makers can use the dashboard to quickly explore performance through interactive filters and visualizations.
